About Cochin Reclaim & Rubbers

Cochin Reclaim & Rubbers is a Kerala-based manufacturer engaged in the processing and production of high-quality reclaimed rubber. Established in 1997, the company has built a strong reputation within the rubber industry for reliability, operational efficiency, and consistent product quality.

The company operates from its manufacturing facility located in the Industrial Development Area at South Vazhakulam, Aluva, in Ernakulam District, Kerala.

Manufacturing & Infrastructure

The production process involves the collection and processing of scrap rubber materials including end-of-life tyres, tread peelings, rubber tubes, and other rubber waste materials. These materials are processed using industry-standard procedures to produce reclaimed rubber sheets of different grades.

The facility is equipped with machinery designed for efficient rubber reclamation and is supported by a trained workforce experienced in industrial rubber processing.

Leadership

The company is led by its Managing Director, Mr. Naeem Vaheed, who brings extensive experience in the rubber reclaim industry. Under his leadership, the company has maintained strong supply relationships with manufacturers in the rubber and tyre industry.

Environmental Responsibility

Reclaimed rubber plays an important role in sustainable manufacturing by enabling the reuse of rubber waste materials that would otherwise contribute to environmental pollution.

Through the processing of discarded tyres, rubber tubes and other rubber waste, Cochin Reclaim & Rubbers contributes to environmentally responsible recycling practices while maintaining high product quality standards.
